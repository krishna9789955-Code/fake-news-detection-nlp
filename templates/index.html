How Our NLP Engine Analyzes News

Unlike naive keyword searchers, our system leverages an end-to-end NLP pipeline designed to prevent statistical leakage and extract semantic writing styles.

01
Leakage Prevention

Removes publisher datelines (e.g. wire service stamps) and eliminates duplicate articles so the classifier evaluates linguistic tone rather than memorizing metadata shortcuts.

02
TF-IDF Vectorization

Extracts 5,000 sublinear TF-IDF features spanning unigrams and bigrams, fitted strictly on the training partition to model stylistic n-gram associations.

03
Calibrated Classifier

Uses L2-regularized Logistic Regression to output well-calibrated class probabilities, validated against Naive Bayes and Linear SVM benchmarks.

04
Local Attribution

Calculates feature contribution products ($w_i \cdot x_i$) to highlight exactly which words and phrases pushed the prediction toward Real or Fake news.
